1. Introduction

Welcome to Sankalp ("the App"), developed by Prince Thakur ("we", "us", or "our"). Sankalp is an all-in-one personal productivity and daily habit tracker designed to help you organize daily routines, track habits, monitor tasks and goals (Sankalps), record personal expenses and debt/credit entries (Udhaar), log workouts and mood check-ins, and synchronize your schedule with Google Calendar.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, where it is stored, how third-party services process it, and how you can exercise control over your personal data. We are committed to transparency and handle personal data in accordance with applicable laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you use the App, as well as limited technical information necessary to operate the service. We only collect data required for the app's core functions:

A. Account and Profile Information

  • Email Address and Display Name: Collected when you register or sign in using email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile Picture URL: If you sign in with Google or update your profile picture, a link to the image is stored.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords for email sign-in are processed securely via Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plaintext.

B. Daily Habits, Tasks, and Goals (Sankalps)

  • Habits: Habit names, frequencies, completion timestamps, and streak counters.
  • Tasks: Task titles, priorities, due dates, completion statuses, and organizational tags.
  • Goals (Sankalps): Target names, target dates, milestones, and progress updates.

C. Financial Tracking (Self-Reported Expenses and Udhaar)

  • Personal Expenses: Transaction amounts, expense categories (e.g., food, utilities, subscriptions), dates, and optional notes entered by you.
  • Udhaar (Personal Debts and Credits): Contact person name, transaction amount, type (money lent or borrowed), due date, notes, and an optional phone number entered manually by you for personal tracking reference.
Notice: Sankalp does not connect to bank accounts, does not access UPI payment rails, does not read SMS messages, and does not process debit or credit card payments. All financial entries are entirely self-reported by you.

D. Wellness, Workouts, and Mood

  • Mood Check-ins: Daily ratings, mood labels/emojis, and optional notes.
  • Workouts: Exercise names, sets, reps, weights, workout durations, and rest timer preferences.
  • Health Metrics: Self-entered metrics such as daily step count, water intake, sleep duration, and weight logged manually in the app.

E. Calendar Data (Optional Integration)

  • If you explicitly connect Google Calendar in the App's Settings, the App accesses your calendar list and events (titles, event start/end times, descriptions, and locations) via the Google Calendar API to display your schedule and sync local events with your Google Calendar.

F. Device and Technical Information

  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens are collected so the backend can deliver scheduled habit and task reminders.
  • App Analytics: Aggregated, pseudonymous usage data (such as app opens, screen views, and device model) collected via Firebase Analytics to ensure app stability.

G. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No precise or background location (GPS) data.
  • No device address book or contact scraping (contacts in Udhaar are entered by you manually).
  • No microphone, camera, or audio recordings.
  • No biometric data or sensitive medical records.
  • No advertising identifier (AAID) tracking for ad targeting.

3. How We Use the Information

The information collected is used strictly to provide and improve the App's functionality:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your personal account session.
  • To save, calculate, and synchronize your habits, tasks, goals, expenses, udhaar records, workouts, and mood entries across your devices.
  • To send push notification reminders for your habits, scheduled tasks, and workout rest timers.
  • To synchronize your events with Google Calendar when you grant permission.
  • To diagnose technical errors, optimize database performance, and improve app stability.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Data Retention

Your personal data is retained in our backend database for as long as your account remains active and registered with Sankalp. Cached offline data is stored locally on your device in application storage.

Routine database backups are stored securely with restricted access and are automatically purged in accordance with our infrastructure backup schedule.

Local Data Wipe vs. Account Deletion

  • Sign Out: Logging out clears your active authentication token from your device. Your data remains safely stored on the backend server for your next login.
  • Clear Data on this Device: Inside the App's Settings, tapping "Clear data on this device" resets local cached records on your phone. This does not alter or erase your cloud-stored data on the backend.
  • Account Deletion: Deleting your account permanently deletes your profile and all associated records (habits, tasks, goals, expenses, udhaar records, workouts, mood logs, and notification tokens) from the database.

7. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application, the FastAPI backend, and third-party APIs are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Secure Authentication: Authenticated requests require valid Firebase JSON Web Tokens (JWT) verified on every API call.
  • Database Access Controls: Database clusters reside in isolated private virtual networks protected by strong credentials and firewall rules.

While we implement robust security measures, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage method is 100% secure. We encourage users to maintain secure device credentials and passwords.

8. Children's Privacy

Sankalp is designed for a general audience (ages 13 and older). The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete such data.

9. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the European Union under GDPR, and California under CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view your personal profile, habits, tasks, workouts, and expense logs directly inside the app.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit your profile name, update habits, or modify entries directly in the app at any time.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can request permanent erasure of your account and personal data as described in Section 6.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in Settings or disable push notifications in your device settings.
